Privacy Policy

Effective 28 October 2026

1. What we collect

When you contact Inrishi, register for a portal, apply as a Partner, or use an Inrishi service, we may collect information such as your name, company, phone number, email address, business requirement, account details, service usage information, files you choose to upload, and payment or project references.

2. Why we use it

We use this information to respond to enquiries, provide and support our software and website services, manage customer and Partner accounts, process service requests, maintain security, improve our products, communicate important account information, and meet legal or compliance obligations.

3. Portal and communication data

Customer and Partner portals may store project information, service records, progress updates, documents, notifications and encrypted chat metadata. Where browser-side encryption is used, message content is encrypted before storage and may not be recoverable on a newly registered device.

4. Google and third-party services

Some services may use Google Workspace, Google Apps Script, Google Drive, Google Sheets, hosting providers, analytics tools, domain providers, email delivery services or other infrastructure needed to operate the service. These providers process data under their own terms and privacy practices.

5. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards including access controls, role-based permissions, session controls, password hashing and OTP verification where enabled. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

6. Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably required to provide services, maintain account and project records, resolve disputes, meet contractual obligations and comply with applicable law.

7. Your choices

You may contact Inrishi to request correction of inaccurate account information or to ask questions about information associated with your account, subject to legal, contractual and operational requirements.

8. Partner applications

Partner applicants provide business and contact details for review. Approved Partners receive account credentials and documents relevant to their Partner relationship. Partner records may be retained for audit, security and commercial administration.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, contact Inrishi through the contact details published on www.inrishi.in.
