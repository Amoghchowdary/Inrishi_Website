Inrishi Partner Terms

Effective 28 October 2026

1. Partner role

An approved Partner identifies genuine business requirements, introduces prospective customers to Inrishi, records accurate lead information and may maintain the business relationship. Inrishi controls official product scope, technical commitments, quotations, discounts, implementation, delivery and support.

2. No unauthorised promises

A Partner must not promise or represent an unapproved price, discount, delivery date, technical capability, guaranteed result, regulatory status or contractual commitment on behalf of Inrishi.

3. Lead quality and consent

Partners should submit genuine opportunities and accurate contact information. The prospect should reasonably expect to be contacted by Inrishi about the stated requirement. Duplicate, speculative, fabricated or unauthorised leads may be rejected.

4. Incentives

Incentive rules are configured service-by-service. Eligibility is linked to attributed customer payments and is subject to Inrishi verification and approval. Partial customer payment may create proportional incentive eligibility where the configured service rule allows it. The Partner Portal is the current record of eligible, approved and paid status.

5. Payment evidence

Partners must record accurate payment references when requested. Inrishi may verify payment receipt and customer attribution before approving or paying an incentive.

6. Confidentiality and data

Partners must protect customer and Inrishi information, use portal access only for authorised business activity and avoid sharing credentials. Customer information must be handled only for legitimate referral and project coordination purposes.

7. Brand use

Partners may use approved Inrishi brochures and materials for genuine referrals. Partners may not alter official pricing, create misleading claims, impersonate Inrishi employees or publish unauthorised statements on behalf of Inrishi.

8. Account status

Inrishi may approve, suspend, deactivate or reject Partner access where necessary for security, compliance, misuse, inaccurate representations or operational reasons.

9. Independent relationship

Partner status does not create employment, agency, franchise or authority to legally bind Inrishi unless a separate written agreement expressly states otherwise.

10. Support

Approved Partners can use the Partner Portal and Admin chat for questions about product fit, customer requirements, payment status and incentive status.
